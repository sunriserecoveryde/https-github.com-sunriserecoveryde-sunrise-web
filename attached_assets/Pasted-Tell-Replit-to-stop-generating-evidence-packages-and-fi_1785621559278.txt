# Phase 2B — Authorization Correction

## 1. Replace flattened permissions and scopes

The current identity model separately unions:

* Permissions
* Roles
* Facility IDs
* Organization-wide status

This allows permission from one role to inherit scope from another role.

Replace it with scoped authorization grants.

Each grant must keep these values together:

* Role assignment ID
* Role
* Permissions granted by that role
* Organization
* Facility or organization scope
* Whether patient assignment is required
* Effective date
* Expiration date
* Revocation status

Authorization must be evaluated through one complete grant.

A permission from one assignment must never inherit organization or facility scope from another assignment.

## 2. Fix Patient List access

Patient List must return only records authorized by the user’s effective grants.

Required behavior:

* Organization-scoped clinical grant: authorized organization patients only
* Facility-scoped clinical grant: assigned facility patients only
* Patient-assignment-limited grant: explicitly assigned patients only
* Multiple valid grants: exact union of independently authorized records
* No frontend-only filtering
* No full facility census for caseload-limited users

Add database-backed tests for BHT, aftercare, clinician, billing, multi-facility, and mixed-role users.

## 3. Add mixed-role exploit tests

Test these exact cases:

1. Organization-wide security administrator plus Facility A BHT
   Must not gain organization-wide patient access.

2. Organization-wide HR plus Facility A clinician
   Clinical access must remain limited to Facility A.

3. Facility A billing plus Facility B BHT
   Billing scope must not bypass BHT patient-assignment restrictions.

4. Facility A clinician plus Facility B billing
   Clinical access must remain limited to Facility A.

5. Organization-wide role without patient permissions
   Must not list or view patients.

6. Expired or revoked broad role plus active restricted role
   Expired or revoked scope must contribute nothing.

7. Unknown role
   Must grant no permissions or scope.

## 4. Prevent role-assignment privilege escalation

The role-management API must not accept arbitrary role strings or arbitrary organization-wide assignments.

Add a central role-grant policy.

Enforce:

* Requested role exists
* Requested scope is allowed for that role
* Administrator is allowed to grant that role
* Facility administrator cannot grant organization-wide roles
* Administrator cannot grant a role above their authority
* User cannot elevate themselves
* User cannot create another account and grant it higher authority
* High-privilege roles require protected organization-level approval
* Target user and facility belong to the same organization
* Role expiration is validated

Add tests proving self-escalation and unauthorized grants fail.
