Do not try to rebuild ADP, Paychex, or Gusto. SunriseOS should manage the healthcare-specific workforce information those systems do not handle well—and integrate with them for payroll, taxes, and benefits.

1. Employee and Contractor Profiles

Each employee profile should contain:

Legal and preferred name
Contact and emergency-contact information
Employment status: employee, contractor, PRN, intern, volunteer
Job title, department, supervisor, location, and levels of care
Hire date, probationary period, anniversary date, and termination date
Full-time, part-time, PRN, salaried, hourly, or contractor classification
Work schedule and availability
Required credentials for the position
Assigned caseload and groups
System roles and EHR access level
Personnel documents
Training and supervision history
Performance reviews and corrective actions
Equipment and system access assigned

Clinical, medical, administrative, recovery-housing, and contractor profiles should have different required fields.

10. Role-Based Access and Offboarding

Onboarding and offboarding should connect directly to SunriseOS security.

When an employee is hired:

Assign position.
Verify credentials.
Complete training.
Approve access.
Assign role-based permissions.
Record devices and equipment.
Activate EHR access.

When an employee leaves:

Record last working date.
Disable SunriseOS access.
Revoke remote access and integrations.
Recover devices, keys, and badges.
Reassign caseload and unfinished documentation.
Transfer tasks and authorizations.
Document final payroll notification.
Preserve the personnel record under the applicable retention schedule.

HHS guidance specifically addresses workforce authorization, clearance, and procedures for ending access to electronic protected health information when employment ends. HHS HIPAA administrative safeguards
