# Sunrise OS — Production Readiness Remediation Program

## Governing Objective

Correct the deficiencies identified in the independent Sunrise OS audit and move the application from a high-fidelity demonstration into a secure, durable, testable, production-capable behavioral-health operating system.

The current application must be treated as:

**An advanced demonstration with strong visual maturity, but incomplete production infrastructure, workflow validation, security controls, integrations, and operational evidence.**

Do not describe Sunrise OS as production-ready, HIPAA-compliant, Part 2-compliant, enterprise-ready, or safe for real patient data until the corresponding readiness gates in this program have been independently verified.

---

# Non-Negotiable Execution Rules

1. Do not attempt this entire program in one agent run.
2. Complete one work package at a time.
3. Use one branch and one commit series per work package.
4. Stop for review after every work package.
5. Do not merge into `main` without explicit approval.
6. Preserve the current demo experience while production foundations are added.
7. Do not use real patient data during remediation.
8. Do not treat polished UI as proof of workflow completion.
9. Do not treat TypeScript or build success as proof of clinical safety.
10. Do not treat browser storage as authoritative clinical persistence.
11. Do not treat client-side permission checks as security enforcement.
12. Do not treat in-memory audit events as a compliant audit trail.
13. Do not claim an integration exists unless it has been tested against a real sandbox or vendor environment.
14. Do not claim zero AI risk.
15. Do not make compliance claims without legal, privacy, clinical, and security review.

---

# Corrected Baseline

Use the following corrected conclusions as the starting point:

* Visual and demonstration maturity is approximately **2.0–2.5 out of 5**.
* Evidence-weighted capability maturity is approximately **0.7 out of 5**.
* Production readiness is approximately **0.5–1.0 out of 5**.
* Sunrise OS is appropriate for controlled demonstrations using fictitious data.
* Sunrise OS is not yet appropriate for real-patient production use.
* No authenticated end-to-end UI workflow has been independently verified.
* Current permission evidence is primarily client-side.
* Clinical and operational persistence is primarily mock, browser-local, or in-memory.
* Durable audit history has not been demonstrated.
* Real external integrations have not been demonstrated.
* Security, backup, disaster recovery, monitoring, and tenant isolation have not been demonstrated.
* The rule-based AI approach may reduce open-ended generation risk, but it does not eliminate clinical, mapping, omission, stale-data, or misclassification risk.

Do not use the prior 2.3/5 overall score as the authoritative readiness score.

# Readiness Gates

## Controlled Demo Gate

Permitted when:

* Only fictitious data is used
* Demo limitations are disclosed
* No production claims are made
* No live payer, pharmacy, or laboratory transactions occur

## Internal QA Gate

Permitted when:

* Persistent test database exists
* Authentication exists
* Server-side permissions exist
* Audit events persist
* Automated tests are running
* No real patient data is used

## Design-Partner Sandbox Gate

Permitted when:

* Security architecture has been reviewed
* Tenant isolation is tested
* Core workflow is end-to-end complete
* Backup and restore are tested
* Support and incident procedures exist
* Data remains synthetic or properly de-identified

## Real-Patient Pilot Gate

Do not enter until all required legal, privacy, security, clinical, operational, and organizational approvals are complete.

## General Production Gate

Do not enter until:

* P0 blockers are closed
* Required P1 blockers are closed
* Independent penetration testing is complete
* Disaster recovery is tested
* Critical workflows pass automated and manual validation
* External integrations are validated
* Executive launch approval is documented
