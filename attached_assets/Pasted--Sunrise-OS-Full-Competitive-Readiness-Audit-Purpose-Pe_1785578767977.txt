# Sunrise OS — Full Competitive Readiness Audit

## Purpose

Perform a comprehensive, evidence-based audit of Sunrise OS to determine whether it has the workflows, capabilities, controls, integrations, usability, and production-readiness required to compete in the behavioral health and substance-use treatment software market.

This is an **audit and documentation task only**.

Do not modify application code, database schemas, permissions, workflows, UI components, routes, seeded data, or configuration.

Do not fix issues during this task.

The objective is to identify:

* What exists
* What is fully functional
* What is partially implemented
* What is represented only by UI or demo data
* What is missing
* What is disconnected
* What is not production-ready
* What requires external integration
* What could differentiate Sunrise OS competitively

Work from the current codebase, running application, existing data model, permissions, routes, tests, configuration, and documentation.

Do not assume a feature is complete because a screen or button exists.

# Audit Standard

Score every capability using this maturity scale:

| Score | Meaning                                                             |
| ----: | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|     0 | Missing                                                             |
|     1 | UI, mockup, placeholder, or demo-only representation                |
|     2 | Partially functional workflow                                       |
|     3 | Complete basic workflow                                             |
|     4 | Integrated, permissioned, auditable, tested, and production-capable |
|     5 | Best-in-class or meaningfully differentiated                        |

Do not award a score of 3 or above unless the workflow can be completed.

Do not award a score of 4 unless evidence confirms:

* Permissions
* Auditability
* Error handling
* Integration
* Testing
* Production-capable behavior

Do not award a score of 5 merely because AI is present.

A score of 5 requires a credible competitive advantage.

# Final Audit Rules

Be critical and conservative.

Do not inflate scores.

Do not hide missing backend behavior behind polished UI.

Do not treat browser-only persistence as enterprise persistence.

Do not treat in-memory audit events as durable audit history.

Do not treat simulated AI delay as a production AI service.

Do not treat mock data as production integration.

Do not treat a hidden navigation item as complete authorization protection.

Do not treat a build passing as proof of clinical safety.

Clearly identify anything that cannot be determined.
