# 5. Complete the Four-Level Finding Taxonomy

Use these exact user-facing labels:

## Critical

Reserve for issues requiring immediate clinician attention before signing.

Examples:

* Wrong patient referenced
* Wrong date, clinician, or program
* Serious safety contradiction
* Conflicting SI/HI documentation
* Significant withdrawal-risk contradiction
* Clinically significant unsupported statement

## Important

Examples:

* Required intervention missing
* Patient response missing
* Medical necessity insufficiently supported
* Continued-service rationale missing
* Major internal inconsistency
* Required note section missing

## Suggested Improvement

Examples:

* Treatment-goal linkage could be clearer
* Follow-up plan could be more specific
* Level-of-care connection could be strengthened
* Clarity or repetition issue
* Professional wording improvement

## Informational

Examples:

* Documentation strength identified
* No consistency concern found
* Medical necessity is supported
* Required field is complete
* Optional formatting guidance
* Review completed without significant concerns

Requirements:

* Do not classify grammar or style findings as Critical.
* Do not classify a positive finding as Important.
* Do not fabricate an Informational concern merely to display all four levels.
* The UI must support all four levels even if a particular review has no findings in one category.
* Hide empty priority sections or show a concise zero-state.
* Communicate priority using text and icon, not color alone.
